Model eval

Runs the labeled eval cases through Gemini Nano (on-device) and Gemini (cloud, redacted pages only) via the offscreen document, then replays both answers through the real triage pipeline to score the hybrid verdict. Cases are written by the same person as the rules, so treat the numbers as a comparison between systems, not real-world accuracy. 


    

      
Scores

      
Lenient counts "phishing" or "suspicious" as a warning; strict counts only "phishing" (the banner). Pages the prefilter skips count as no warning. A failed model call counts as no warning for that model alone.
